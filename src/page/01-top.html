How to read this atlas

India's data centres in ten numbers

The second edition of the atlas: five parts, nineteen chapters, one scenario that every tool follows, and a brief mode that cuts it to a ten-minute read. Start with the ten numbers, watch the map, then follow the parts in order or jump to a question.

Every headline figure carries a range or a dated source. Every chart, map and diagram has a yellow How to read this button that explains what it shows and what it means. Shared values come from one standards register (version 2026.10.6), each with an owner and a review date.
