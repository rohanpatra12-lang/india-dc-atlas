Where to build, and at what cost

The questionWhich Indian hubs suit which data centres, and what does each cost to build and run?

The short answerMumbai leads on connectivity; Hyderabad, Chennai and Visakhapatnam on land and power. A colocation hall costs about ₹56 crore per MW to build (₹50–62); an AI campus about ₹340 crore per MW with GPUs (₹310–490, mostly GPU prices).

How this is calculated · city ranking (Steps A–E)
  1. A · Hard filters: keep a city only if seismic zone ≤ IV, nearest 220 kV+ substation ≤ your distance limit, not very-high cyclone or flood (if that filter is on), and groundwater not over-exploited unless treated water is assumed
  2. Latency (ms) = mean over Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Bengaluru of 2 × great-circle km × 1.4 ÷ 200 = 0.014 × km
  3. B · Min–max: s = 10 × (x − min) ÷ (max − min) over the cities that pass the filters; latency flipped: s = 10 × (max − x) ÷ (max − min)
  4. B · Report rubric (alternative): factor scores as published in Report 10; latency = 10 × (26 − RTT) ÷ 16, clipped 0–10
  5. C · Weighted sum: score = Σ wᵢ × sᵢ with Σ wᵢ = 1 (sliders are rescaled to sum to 1)
  6. C · Geometric: score = Π max(sᵢ, 0.5)^wᵢ (floor 0.5 so a zero does not wipe out the city)
  7. Tiers: A = top 30% of cities that pass, B = next 40%, C = rest; excluded cities shown separately
  8. E · Monte Carlo: 1,000 runs; each weight × U(0.8, 1.2) then rescaled; each factor score × U(0.8, 1.2), clipped 0–10; count how often each city is top 3 in its state and top 10 nationally
Assumptions
  • Power score = 50% tariff + 50% grid and renewables judgement (Report 10). Connectivity, water, climate, hazards, land, policy and demand are Report 10 rubric scores (0–10).
  • Substation names and distances are estimates; spare capacity is unpublished everywhere.
  • Effective tariff = energy charge + demand charge ÷ 584 h (80% load factor).
  • Seismic zones follow IS 1893:2016 (the 2025 revision was withdrawn in March 2026).
  • The Monte Carlo uses a fixed seed, so results repeat until you press "Re-run".

Before you shortlist a site

  • Grid headroom is the binding unknown. Ask the state transmission utility (MSETCL, TANTRANSCO, KPTCL, TGTRANSCO, APTRANSCO, UPPTCL, HVPNL and so on) for a load-flow study, and CTUIL for interstate connectivity margins.
  • Water: get the block-level category from IN-GRES (CGWB) and a signed treated-water offtake with the city water board before you rely on any water score.
  • Hazards: check the plot against the NRSC Bhuvan flood atlas and NDMA cyclone zones; the ratings here are city-level.
  • Climate: compute free-cooling hours from ISHRAE/ASHRAE typical-year files; the values here are approximations.
  • Announcements are not assets: Vizag's 5 GW, the Digha cable landing and Jamnagar are plans.

What the numbers say to do

  • Colo developers: underwrite to ₹7,700–8,200/kW/month or a facility cost ≤₹50 crore/MW; phase fit-out with leasing; push interconnection density.
  • Hyperscale build-to-suit is the best risk-adjusted return (~14–15%); lock PUE caps and power pass-through in contracts.
  • GPU buyers: don't buy without ≥60–70% contracted utilisation. Below ~55%, renting at ₹218–256/hr beats owning an H100. Model at your real WACC, and budget the IGST working-capital drag.
  • Investors: pay up for secured power, land banks and pre-leases, the scarce inputs, not concrete and chillers.
