The questionWhat changes when the racks are built for AI?

AI racks are a different machine

A conventional rack draws about as much as a few homes; a GB200 rack draws about as much as a street. That one fact changes cooling, power delivery, grid planning and cost. Per query the footprint is small. The aggregate is not.

Load profilesWhy grids now treat AI training as a reliability risk.

NERC documented a July 2024 event where ~1,500 MW of data-centre load switched to backup at once after a 230 kV fault. Mitigations: GPU power smoothing, rack storage, site batteries, ride-through.

India's AI build-out

  • IndiaAI Mission: ₹10,372 crore; 38,231 GPUs onboarded across 14 providers at an average ≈₹65/GPU-hour (March 2026); ~20,000 more announced.
  • Yotta: NM1 Navi Mumbai, Tier IV, 52 MW, 8,192 H100s; B300 and Vera Rubin plans at Greater Noida (announced, not commissioned).
  • Reliance Jamnagar: up to 3 GW plan; 120 MW+ expected online H2 2026; Meta lease ~168 MW.
  • Sify: first Indian DGX-Ready liquid-cooling certification (130 kW/rack), later 200 kW/rack sites.
  • Retrofit reality: a 10× density jump strands floor space, needs new transformers, water loops, >1.3 t floor loading and taller halls. Build liquid-ready shells now.

Anatomy: from the substation to the chipTwo paths run through every AI data centre: electricity comes in, heat goes out. Water enters where the heat leaves (cooling towers) and, out of sight, at the power stations that make the electricity.
