Caveats and sources

  • The worked cases (20 MW colo, 50 MW hyperscale, 100 MW AI) are bottom-up estimates built on published benchmarks, not audited project data. Water balances are modelled; real sites can differ ±30–50%.
  • There is no public national dataset of Indian data-centre PUE, WUE or clean-energy share. Bengaluru sector water estimates range from about 2 to 20 MLD.
  • Contested figures: GB300 rack price (US$3.7–6.5 m), GPU economic life (4–6 years), Rubin chip power, hydro water intensity, marginal emission factors, Karnataka's consumption-mix intensity, Andhra Pradesh's 70% renewable rule (a ministerial statement).
  • Scarcity weights and AWARE-style factors are illustrative; rankings hold, absolute values do not.
  • Several 2026 state policies are known from cabinet notes and press; gazette numbers were not located for Karnataka, UP and Gujarat 2026.
  • The state index, maturity levels, Karnataka hourly curve and city design temperatures are indicative tools for comparison, not published datasets.
  • Chapter 8: climate hours, peak factors and hourly curves are models built on approximate climate normals, not measurements; replace with ISHRAE typical-year data and metering. Several tariffs come from secondary summaries; MSEDCL's energy charge is disputed (₹7.48 in the MERC press note versus a reported ₹8.68).
  • Calculators simplify: they hold demand charges, overhead splits and other costs at each report's assumptions, so results can differ slightly from report tables.
