# Research reports

The atlas is compiled from a 32-part research series and three models. Reports 1–15 are the original series; Reports 16–32 are the later research notes the Reference page lists by subject. Chapters cite Reports 1–15 by number. The Reference page lists the later notes by subject; where it names the chapter that uses one, that chapter is shown below, and "—" means the note informed the atlas more generally.

The atlas standards register (`src/data/standards.js`) is authoritative. Reports record what the research found at the time, and some early figures were later corrected (most importantly India grid water 4.5 → 2.9 L/kWh and grid CO₂ 0.71 → 0.675 t/MWh in October 2026).
