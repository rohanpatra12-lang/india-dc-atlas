    How it is calculated

    • Capacity is live IT load. It grows at a constant annual rate between the 2026, 2030 and 2035 values. The workload split applies the AI share, then the training share within AI.
    • Energy = IT MW × 8,760 h × utilisation × occupancy × PUE, for each workload.
    • Cooling: each fleet (general-purpose or AI, existing or new-build) is a mix of two systems from the cooling table. A system's PUE and WUE are averaged over the climate mix. The 2026 fleet's share of a year's capacity = 2026 capacity × (1 − retrofit rate)^years ÷ that year's capacity; the rest uses the new-build mix.
    • On-site water = IT energy × fleet WUE.
    • Power-station water = grid share of facility energy × grid water intensity. Renewable supply bought under contract is counted as needing no water.
    • Carbon = grid energy × grid emission factor.
    • Annual cost of ownership = facility and land capex spread over the facility's life + IT hardware with import duty spread over the refresh cycle (both at the cost of capital, using a capital recovery factor) + electricity + water + O&M + staff + insurance and property tax. All figures are in constant 2026 rupees.
    • Capex spent that year = new MW × (facility + land + IT with duty) + IT refresh of the existing fleet (fleet ÷ refresh years).
